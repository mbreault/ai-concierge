# Find it. Build it. Run it.

Version: synthesis v3 · Date: 2026-09-14 · Editor: Codex, synthesizing the ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude discussion.

**Status: proposed working offer for Mike's review.** Prices are USD. This is an iteration document, not a published rate card or signed service agreement. Primary basis: [ChatGPT's final post-Claude recommendation](../../research/sources/chatgpt-discussion.md#turn-4), with [Claude v2](claude-offerings-v2.md) supplying draft deliverables. Where the discussion did not settle a term, it remains open below.

Sit with someone, find the workflow that hurts, get them using the tools, then build the thing that runs it — and stay on if they want.

Thirty years of consulting and software development, applied to making AI useful inside the systems your business already runs. The work includes data, permissions, integrations, testing and the judgment to know which steps need predictable software or a person.

## Find it

**Fit call — free, 20 minutes.** Find out whether there is a workflow worth investigating and whether we are a good fit.

**Tools Working Session — $500, 90 minutes, one person.** Work with AI tools on a real task and identify the next useful workflow. Claude proposed crediting this toward the audit; whether to adopt that additional credit is still open.

**Workflow Audit — $2,500.** Fixed scope agreed before starting; plan on two weeks, subject to access and stakeholder availability. Proposed deliverables, adapted from Claude:

- A map of how the selected workflow operates today, including handoffs and recurring problems.
- Ranked opportunities using recoverable time, business value, feasibility and risk, with assumptions visible.
- Specific tool recommendations with reasons and costs to verify.
- A bounded prototype of the top candidate where agreed in scope, clearly identified as a prototype.
- A written 30/60/90-day plan with owners and a 60-minute readout.

**Credit the full $2,500 audit fee toward a build started within 90 days.** This is the proposed commercial mechanism from the discussion. Define the start of the 90-day clock, qualifying build, deposit/invoice treatment and credit stacking in the quote. Do not promise a fixed page count; the deliverable must support a build decision.

The “10 recoverable hours/month or you don't pay” guarantee is a **candidate pilot experiment**, not included in the default offer. Measurement, exclusions and refund/payment mechanics need to be agreed before it is used.

### Proposed support categories

| Category | What it means | Commercial treatment to specify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fix | Delivered behavior fails to meet the agreed specification | Covered defects, stabilization period, exclusions and supported systems |
| Platform Adaptation | An upstream model, API or platform change requires changes to retain existing agreed behavior | A bounded monthly allowance; Claude proposed two hours, but the current allowance is undecided. Define overage approval/rate and unused-hour treatment. |
| New work | A new feature, workflow, integration or materially expanded behavior | Separate quote or agreed engineering capacity |

### Proposed response targets

Coverage proposal: **Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern**, with holidays, absence coverage and escalation channel to be specified before sale. These targets would apply only to systems on an agreed support plan.

| Severity | Example | Initial response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | System down; nobody can use the supported workflow | 4 business hours |
| P2 | Material degradation or a burdensome workaround | 1 business day |
| P3 | Minor defect, cosmetic issue or question | 3 business days |

Response means a human assessment, a stated next step and an estimate where possible. An automatic receipt is not the response. Resolution depends on the incident and third-party dependencies; do not offer a universal resolution time or guarantee uptime of Microsoft, OpenAI, Anthropic or other external infrastructure.

For clarity, four business hours from Friday at 4:30 p.m. ends Monday at **11:30 a.m.** under this proposed window, assuming Monday is a covered day. This corrects the arithmetic in the preserved Claude source.

Response-credit remedies, business-day counting rules, planned maintenance, third-party outages and client-caused incidents remain contract-design questions. Claude's automatic 10% credit capped at 30% is an alternative to assess, not an adopted commitment.
