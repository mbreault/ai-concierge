## Hardware evolution within this stage

Turn 2 proposed an off-menu **Executive AI Desk from $7,500 setup + $750/month**, with hardware incidental, a manufacturer's warranty and no included onsite break/fix. It recommended launching the core business without the appliance and testing it as a demo/special offer.

In turn 3, Mike explicitly agreed that hardware brought substantial liability and should be roughly ten times the original price. ChatGPT then moved the concept to **$15,000+ deployment + $1,500–$2,500/month**, with separately scoped onsite work and a clarified responsibility boundary. The later post-Claude reply widens the monthly range to $1,000–$2,500. The premium/off-menu direction carries forward; an exact recurring price was not agreed, and the original low-cost offer remains in O1.
