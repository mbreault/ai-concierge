# Gemini proposal — editorial extraction

Archived: 2026-09-14. Source author: Gemini. Extraction editor: Codex. Status: historical alternative, not approved.

This is a comparison-friendly extraction, **not a verbatim standalone Gemini offering sheet**. The [complete original analysis](../../research/sources/gemini-analysis.md), especially “Restructured Service Architecture and Productized Rate Card,” is authoritative for Gemini's wording.

## Diagnose → Align → Build → Manage

Position the practice around senior software engineering and integrating AI into business systems. Raise entry prices, separate advisory from production maintenance, and drop the low-cost physical appliance in favor of cloud/browser delivery.

| Offer | Proposed price | Scope in the source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Opportunity & Systems Audit | $950 | 90-minute diagnostic; workflow/data/security readiness and deployment roadmap; full credit against an implementation started within 30 days |
| SMB Team Enablement Sprint | $3,500 | Four hours, up to 12 participants; practical workflows and an automation backlog |
| Executive AI Strategy Intensive | $10,000–$12,500 | Full day; governance, vendor choice and implementation roadmap |
| Production Model & API Integration | $4,500 | Fixed-scope integration sprint with authentication, validation, logging, rate handling and fallbacks |
| Enterprise Copilot Studio Agent | $7,500–$12,500 | Tenant setup, data grounding and scoped integrations |
| Custom engineering | $8,500 per one-week sprint, or $250/hour | Working, documented and tested technical milestones |
| AI Advisory Concierge | $1,500/month | Two 45-minute reviews, priority async communication and prompt-library review |
| Managed AI Systems | $4,500–$5,500/month | 15–20 engineering hours for maintenance and small changes, with severity-based response commitments |
| Fractional Chief AI Officer | $8,500–$12,500/month | Architecture, governance, security and vendor leadership |
| Physical Chief of Staff | Remove | Avoid onsite device support and its effect on margins |

The source is not perfectly consistent: an earlier corrective table gives a managed tier of $4,500+, the ladder shows $5,500, and the narrative gives $4,500–$5,500. The diagram also shows an audit range of $950–$1,500, while the detailed offer is $950. Those differences are preserved here rather than silently harmonized.

## Support direction

Use bounded engineering capacity, monitoring and explicit severity-based terms instead of $100/month break/fix. The advisory proposal mentions a 24-hour async response SLA; the research also explores enterprise-style response and uptime frameworks. These are Gemini's historical recommendations, not promises adopted by the current draft. See the [support decision](../../decisions/README.md#d009-sla).
