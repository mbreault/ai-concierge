# Offering comparison

Updated 2026-09-14. **Current version: [AI Concierge v4](versions/2026-09-14-ai-concierge-v4.md).** The user selected AI Concierge branding and an initial paid call below $1,000. V4 proposes a $750 diagnostic within that ceiling; the exact price remains a proposal. Hardware liability and substantially higher pricing also have explicit user support. See the [feedback](../brainstorming/2026-09-14-positioning-and-entry-feedback.md) and [decision log](../decisions/README.md).

The five-column tables preserve the discussion through **historical post-Claude synthesis v3**. They are followed by a compact v3 → v4 change table. Prices have different scopes and are not independently verified market benchmarks or approved price lists.

Sources: [original v1](versions/original-v1.md), [Gemini analysis](../research/sources/gemini-analysis.md), [ChatGPT after Gemini](../research/sources/chatgpt-discussion.md#turn-2), [Claude v2](versions/claude-offerings-v2.md), and [synthesis v3](versions/2026-09-14-synthesis-v3.md), based on [ChatGPT after Claude](../research/sources/chatgpt-discussion.md#turn-4).
