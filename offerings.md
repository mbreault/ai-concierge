# AI Concierge

Sit with someone, find the workflow that hurts, get them using the tools, then build the thing that runs it — and stay on if they want.

## 1. Start

Paid discovery. Cheap on purpose.

- **Assessment** — 30 min, one workflow. $100.
- **Tools session** — 60 min, Claude / ChatGPT / NotebookLM. $200.

Assessment finds the work. Tools session makes them dangerous enough to care. They leave knowing what to automate.

## 2. Workshop

The team version of step 1. A paid discovery call with the whole room. The first build reveals itself when someone elbows a coworker and says “we could do this for our quoting process.”

- **Public workshop** — 4 hours, $500/head. Same job as the tools session, in a room. Cheap enough that people walk out calling it a bargain.
- **Company workshop** — half a day with the team. $15,000. Hands-on: get them on Claude, build one skill together, ask what eats hours of their week. Walk out with the first agent to pitch.

## 3. Stay

A regular partner before or after a build.

- **Enterprise** — two 45-minute sessions a month. $1,000/month.

Not more teaching. The monthly slot where the next agent gets scoped.

## 4. Build

The real product, revealed by step 1. Pick one.

- **Model connections** (Claude / OpenAI) — $500 + $100/month.
- **Copilot Studio agent** — $5,000 per agent.
- **Custom development** — $200/hour.
- **AI Chief of Staff** — kiosk in the office (Raspberry Pi or similar). $1,500 + $200/month.

Monthly support (connections and Chief of Staff): 48-hour email. Break/fix, keep it running. New work is a new build or hourly.
