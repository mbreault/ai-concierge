## Evidence and corrections

- **Missing report:** the initial ChatGPT deep-research answer was absent from the returned conversation. Later mentions of AgenTomte, Nova3, Yotta and Intraeli remain attributed conversation claims; this archive cannot reproduce or independently validate that missing research.
- **Research date:** source files were imported on 2026-09-14. Claude's report states September 2026. Import date is not a claim that all linked vendor prices were checked that day.
- **Market generalizations:** claims such as every serious discovery offer crediting forward, a retainer-market “dead zone,” or no published Platform Adaptation clauses are the source author's conclusions, not established universals. Treat them as hypotheses to test.
- **Known SLA arithmetic issue:** Claude v2 says a Friday 4:30 p.m. P1 is due Monday noon. Under its 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern window, four business hours end Monday **11:30 a.m.**, assuming no holiday. The historical file remains unchanged; authored support notes use the corrected calculation.
- **Vendor charges:** Claude's Microsoft licensing, capacity and message/credit examples are historical. The working offer states that vendor costs are separate and must be checked for the client's tenant and usage; it does not republish those numbers as current.
- **Existing snippets:** the root training excerpt and firm-engagement note have incomplete attribution. Preserve them as leads for research, not independently established revenue results or portfolio claims.

## Adding research

Archive the source, record author/date/origin, and distinguish a published vendor price from a quote, a survey, anecdotal evidence or model inference. Add corrections beside the source rather than rewriting it. Link a recommendation to the exact source section and describe what remains uncertain before it becomes a client-facing claim.
