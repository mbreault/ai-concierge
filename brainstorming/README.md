## Questions for the next iteration

1. **Buyer and niche:** Claude targets owner-operated SMBs at $1M–$20M revenue; Gemini's suggested ranges vary, including 10–100 employees / $3M–$30M. Choose a test segment based on existing relationships and workflow knowledge instead of treating either model's range as settled.
2. **Launch menu:** Which few offers should be prominent first? V4 replaces the entry path with the proposed $750 diagnostic and preserves the remaining post-Claude offers for review; optional hardware and fractional leadership remain off the core launch list.
3. **Discovery economics:** Bound the proposed $750 diagnostic and written recommendation under Mike's $1,000 ceiling. Resolve the credit clock and tools-session credit stacking. Treat the $2,500 deeper audit as a historical alternative, not a required first step.
4. **Recurring economics:** What are the included sessions/hours and response obligations? Cost the allowance and absence coverage before selling a small Care plan or a dedicated retainer.
5. **Workshop operations:** Who owns recruitment, venue, travel, preparation, consent for follow-up and participant materials? Define the sponsor package before pricing individual seats.
6. **Case studies:** Which prior engagements can Mike substantiate and share? The root firm-engagement snippet is not established as his work and must not become a testimonial or case study by inference.
7. **Missing context:** Recover the original ChatGPT research report if available; improve attribution for the training excerpt and truncated firm note. The four requested attachments are already archived.
